How to play

  • Draft 1 of 3 market cards each round — Bramble takes one too.
  • Brew one set per round from your hand: a pair (+potency), a trio (+3), a prism of 3 suits (+4), a quad (+8).
  • Overbrew once a round: draw 2 extra cards — but a Fizzle halves the points you brewed this round.
  • First to 30, or the best score after 8 rounds, takes the Golden Ladle.
